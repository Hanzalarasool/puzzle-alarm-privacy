Privacy Policy — Puzzle Alarm

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version. Puzzle Alarm does not collect anything. Your alarms, reminders, prayer times and tasks are stored on your phone and never sent to me or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no adverts.

Who this is about

Puzzle Alarm (package com.hanza.puzzlealarm) is an Android app published by an individual developer. In this policy, “the app” means Puzzle Alarm and “I” means that developer. Contact details are at the bottom.

What the app stores

All of it stays in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • Alarms — time, repeat days, label, puzzle and step settings
  • Reminders — title, time, repeat days
  • Prayer times — the time you set for each of the five prayers and how far ahead to be notified
  • Tasks — title, schedule, and which days you ticked them off
  • The daily review time, if you switch that reminder on
  • Water reminders — the window you chose and how often to be nudged
  • Whether the one-time unlock has been purchased

None of this is transmitted off your device by the app. There is no server behind Puzzle Alarm, and I have no way to see any of it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing is collected

Permissions, and why each one is needed

Backups

There are two ways your data can leave the app's private storage, and you control both:

  • Android's own backup. If you have Android backup switched on, the system copies the app's settings to your own Google account so a reinstall restores them. This is a platform feature between you and Google — it is encrypted, it goes to your account, and I have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.
  • The backup file. Backup & restore inside the app writes your data to a file in a location you pick. That file is yours; the app does not upload it anywhere. It deliberately does not contain purchase information.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment is made to Google, not to me — I never see your card, billing address or any other payment detail. The app only ever learns whether the item has been purchased. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Getting in touch from inside the app

The side menu has a Support on WhatsApp link. Tapping it hands the support number to WhatsApp, or to your browser if WhatsApp is not installed — the app does not send anything itself and has no idea whether you went on to write a message. Anything you do send is a normal WhatsApp conversation, covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy rather than this one, and I will see whatever your WhatsApp account normally shows a recipient — your phone number, and your profile name and photo if you have them.

This is the only link in the app that goes anywhere outside it. There are no adverts and nothing else that opens another app or a web page.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can also clear it from Android's app settings, or delete individual alarms, reminders and tasks in the app. Since nothing is collected, there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
